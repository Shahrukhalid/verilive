Verilive: free deepfake detector for video calls

Is the face on your Zoom, Google Meet or Microsoft Teams call real? Verilive is a free, open-source deepfake check that runs entirely in your web browser. It looks for a pulse in the skin, natural blinking and the tells of a face swap, and gives each person on the call a risk reading. Your video never leaves your computer.

How it works

  • Share the call window, open a recording, or test yourself with your camera.
  • Six signals: pulse in skin colour, blinking, face sharpness match, colour flicker, face shape stability and a guided head-turn test.
  • Each person gets a verdict: looks real, some warning signs, or likely synthetic.
